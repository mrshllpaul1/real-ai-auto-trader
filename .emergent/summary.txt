**Key Technical Concepts**
-   **Rainbow DQN:** The new core AI, combining 7 key DRL improvements (DDQN, Dueling, PER, C51, Noisy Nets, N-step learning).
-   **Transformer Encoder:** Used within the Rainbow network to process sequences of market data, superior to LSTMs for capturing long-range dependencies.
-   **C51 Distributional RL:** The agent predicts a full distribution of future returns, not just the mean, allowing for more nuanced risk assessment.
-   **WebSocket:** Used for a persistent, low-latency connection to Kraken for real-time order book data.
-   **SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations):** A game-theoretic approach to explain the output of any machine learning model, used here to justify trade decisions.
-   **Genetic Algorithms (DEAP/Optuna):** Used to perform evolutionary optimization on the trading system's hyperparameters.
-   **MLflow:** A platform for managing the ML lifecycle, including experiment tracking, reproducibility, and deployment. To be implemented.
